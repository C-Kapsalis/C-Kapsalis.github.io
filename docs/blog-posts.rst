A three-part series on Power BI data models, from reviewing one to
governing many to letting the business design its own:

:doc:`What to look for in a data model <what-to-look-for-in-a-data-model>`
   *October 2026* · A review checklist for Power BI semantic models, grouped
   by layer, with the reason behind every item: the defects that stop a
   project opening, the relationship and DAX errors that stay quiet, and the
   report-layer problems that only show up in someone else's hands.

:doc:`One governed model, kept as text <one-governed-model-kept-as-text>`
   *October 2026* · Why a central, governed data model needs a text format,
   how TMDL turns modeling best practice into something you can review,
   test, and transfer between models, and the mechanisms that make
   transfer safe across a fleet.

:doc:`From business idea to data model, without the queue <democratizing-data-model-mock-ups>`
   *October 2026* · How I built modeling knowledge into pbip-model-forge so
   domain experts can design working data models themselves, shortening the
   path from business ideation to data-team delivery.

:doc:`Integration over destination <integration-over-destination>`
   *July 2026* · Why wellness programs, and most tools, fail when they build
   new destinations and expect people to come, instead of meeting people where
   their attention already lives. The thinking behind CleanLeaf, generalized to
   marketing and product design.
